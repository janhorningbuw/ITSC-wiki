To publish your PhD thesis you have to send three printed copies (if you want by post), additionally to the ones you provided when you handed in your thesis, to Mrs. Grimberg (grimberg@uni-wuppertal.de). Moreover, Mrs. Grimberg needs an e-mail from Tibor confirming that he and the second examiner allowed you to publish your thesis.

Additionally, you have to provide another three printed copies of your thesis (also via post if you want to) to library, see for https://elekpub.bib.uni-wuppertal.de/ubwhs for a checklist and instructions. The digital version has to be uploaded here: https://elekpub.bib.uni-wuppertal.de/ubwhs/document/create/doctoralThesis. You also need to provide the filled-out form https://elekpub.bib.uni-wuppertal.de/ubwhs/domainresource/formbl.pdf, which has to be signed physically (!).
