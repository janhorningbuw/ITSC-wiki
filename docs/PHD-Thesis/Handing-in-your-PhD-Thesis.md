When you hand in your PhD-thesis you will have to follow the process below.

# The process of handing in your thesis
1. First, you will need a second examiner for your thesis. Usually, external researchers you published a paper with are good candidates. Also coordinate this with Tibor.
1. Make an appointment for the date of your defense with [Mrs Grimberg](https://www.lfeb.uni-wuppertal.de/de/personen/detail/grimber/). Note that you can only defend your thesis on **Fridays during the lecture period at 3 pm**, and that each Friday only one candidate can defend their thesis. So make the appointment rather early. Mrs Grimberg will offer you a couple of possible dates in a range of your choice that you will then have to coordinate with Tibor and your second examiner. (Remark: In exceptional cases it is also possible to deviate from the Firday 3 pm slot. In this case, the PhD student has to coordinate/organize rooms etc. themselves.)
2. You will also have to make an appointment with Mrs Grimberg to hold a public colloquium, in which you present your thesis. This should happen on a **Wednesday in the lecture period at 2 pm**. You may also deviate from this date and time, but then you have to **organize a room** for your colloquium. Note that you can hold your colloquium well in advance of your defense, however, it can be useful to hold it shortly before your defense to practice your talk with an audience.
3. You will have to hand in your thesis (together with a bunch of other documents listed below) at least **3 month** before your defense to [Mrs Grabowski](https://ihct.uni-wuppertal.de/de/team/sekretariat/martina-grabowski.html), **not** Mrs Grimberg. Coordinate a time and date with her to hand in your thesis, to make sure she is in the office when you hand in your thesis. Mrs Grabowski is responsible for this because Prof. Dr. rer. nat. Pfeiffer is head of the doctoral committee and she is his secretary. Make sure to update this page if this changed.

# List of documents to hand in with your thesis (translated from German, original below)
**The start of the promotion process is applied for in writing to the chairman of the doctoral committee, currently Prof. Dr. rer. nat. Ullrich Pfeiffer. 
1. a curriculum vitae written in German, outlining in particular the course of studies.
2. the certificate of general higher education entrance qualification (Abitur certificate) - certified copy
3. the certificate of the successfully completed Master degree. The list states you need a certificate that you passed your "Diplom", but what they mean is your Master's degree. Also the form states you also need to submit a copy of the Bachelor's degree, but the Master's degree is sufficient. - certified copy
4. the invitation to the electrotechnical colloquium talk
5. three **bound** dissertations (one for each reviewer, one for the files)
6. one **electronic version** of the dissertation, either on a CD/DVD or a USB flash drive
7. a declaration that the submitted thesis is independently written - see appendix 1 to the form
8. a declaration that only the aids specified in the dissertation have been used in the writing of the work and that all passages taken over verbatim or in content have been marked as such - see appendix 1 to the form
9. a declaration that the dissertation in its current or another version has not already been submitted to another department of a scientific university - see appendix 1 to the form
10. a certificate stating that the applicant was still enrolled at least 3 months ago or is enrolled at least in the semester in which the doctoral examination takes place - available for download in Studilöwe
10. a certificate that the applicant is employed by the university. This can simply be a copy of your work contract. Only in case you are not an employee, you have to provide a certificate of good conduct of the type "behördliches Führungszeugnis", which you can get at the "Einwohnermeldeamt" and which they should send directly to the address of the doctoral committee chairperson.
11. a list of the scientific writings published by the applicant to date
12. optional suggestions for members of your committee. Enter your second examiner in appendix 2 of the form. Giving further suggestions how to form the committee is usually not "possible". However, for chairing the committee there will be a ET research group selected. Maybe talk to Tibor here, usually here suggestions can be considered. The other members are first and second examaniner (Tibor + ?), another CS professor and a PostDoc of CS. 
13. optional confirmation that non-members of the committee can be there for your exam - see appendix 2 to the form

## Remarks regarding the list
* You can get a certified copy of an official document for example at the dean's office, BUW's AStA (without any charge), or at your local "Einwohnermeldeamt". Usually it will be easiest if you just bring your documents and let them make the copies before certifying them. That way, they are sure that the copies are correct.
