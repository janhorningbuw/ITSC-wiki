Your PhD defense appointment usually is the first thing that you should reserve with Mrs. Grimberg as this determines all subsequent deadlines (incl. your thesis submission deadline). It has to take place on a Friday in the lecture period. If all Friday spots are taken, then Wednesday spots are given as a backup.

**Important:** The default language of the exam is **German**. In some circumstances you can apply for an English exam by talking to the chair of the committee, but there has to be a good reason (e.g., you or your second examiner are not fluent in German). 

For me (Pascal) this was different. While scheduling the date for my defense with Mrs. Grimberg she said to me its no problem that the defense is in English (second examiner does not speak german), but I should emphasize this if I hand in my thesis. Also, I could freely choose among the available dates, there was no preference between Wednesday or Friday spots. I feel like there is always a bit of chaos going on :(.

# Talking to your Committee

* Make sure that you keep updated on the selection of your comittee, because  it is usual that you offer a chat to each member of the comittee from BUW before your defense. This information can be fetched from Mrs. Grimberg. Usually this is announced in the spreadsheet sent around to inform about the upcoming defenses. Only Jutta, Tibor, and Kai receive this, so before asking Mrs. Grimberg, ask Jutta first.

* 4-6 weeks before your defense you should send an E-Mail to each of your committee members. The mail mostly serves as a kind introduction to yourself, but you should also offer a personal chat. Many people like to talk to you before your defense (a remnant from the Rigorosium days) and this is also very helpful for you. They will tell you what they are doing and you could deduce possible intersections (and thus possible questions) from this. The mail roughly should contain:

  * The date of your defense
  * Short intro of yourself and your thesis (member of ITSC with Tibor, title of the thesis, brief summary of the thesis (~2 sentences)) 
  * Attach the abstract and Zusammenfassung (if present), maybe also your list of publications
  * **important** Offer to chat with them (Tip: I would try to offer in person primarily, but you can always suggest Zoom as an alternative. Some are more traditional, so Zoom should not be the first choice.) Some (Tutsch for example) also do not want to talk to you beforehand, except if you insist on this. I (Pascal) didn't, and I advise you also not to insist on this, if the person politely declines your offer.

* Do not worry about the chat and do not prepare like crazy. Maybe inform yourself a bit about the person before the meeting to find possible intersection right away. But be careful not to overly make the impression that you know about the field of the committee member, because this might be seen as an "OK" to ask questions in their comfort zone during your defense.
* Do not prepare slides, or bring the whole defense talk, but make sure you have your thesis at hand (either a printed copy or digitally). First, it is more natural if you just stop by to chat rather then giving a talk. Second, they will be bored during your defense if you say the same stuff again. Be natural and kind, the overall impression you leave is also important!


# Defense Prep

* The defense usually will take place in the dean's meeting room (FME 01.04). You need to fetch a key from the dean's office. They were kind enough to ask me (Denis) when I will come to fetch the key, but of course it is always better to be proactive. In the past the student had to take care of the booking of the room, so if you want to be extra sure, then maybe ask in the dean's office early enough whether it was booked for you.
* It is common and somewhat expected to have drinks and snacks for the comittee. David, Kai, Jan, Denis, Tobias and Jonas had water, coffee and sweets for everyone. Do not go crazy, it is just a kind gesture. There are cups, etc. in the room. You can use them and put them in the dishwasher in the dean's kitchen afterwards. Do *not* turn on the dishwasher.
* The tables have to be arranged specifically as shown on the picture below. Each member has its own table and the first and second examiner sit right in front of you, the others to the sides. There is a variation to this that Jan was told to do, where there is two desks for 3rd and 4th examiners to the side of the door and one desk for the head of the committee on the side of the windows.

# Defense Schedule

The defense has a very special schedule.

1. 15:00: Everyone will meet in the room, the chair of the committee says "Hello" and then you will be asked to leave the room. Make sure there are people waiting for you in front of the room to talk about other stuff and calm down a bit.
2. While you are outside, the committee will talk about your thesis, the examiners present the reviews, etc. This will take about 15 min.
3. 15:15: You will be called back and then you have 30 mins (!) to give your talk. Make sure that you do not talk longer then this. Depending on the chair, he might simply interrupt you. A bit less is fine, but try to stay in the 28-30 min range. Tip: Use the last slide to your advantage, either just skip through, or elaborate a bit on the summary. Also keep a watch at hand, Denis used a stopwatch to monitor the time secretly. Also try to write down the time you take to reach some checkpoints of your talk while practicing the talk. Then you know during the defense whether you are on time, or a bit too slow or too fast.
4. 15:45: Questions begin. 20 mins each for your 1st and 2nd reviewer, then 35-50 mins for the other three, the head of the committee will go last. For Denis it was roughly 1:15. 
5. 17:00-17:15: You need to leave the room again and the committee discusses your grade. This takes as much time as they need. Again, have someone waiting for you to calm you down.
6. Then you will be called back in, they will tell you that you cannot call yourself a doctor yet without receiving the PhD certificate from the head of the university. Finally they announce the grade, handing you a paper declaring you passed the defense with your grade on it. Shake hands, you did it. If you prepared a reception, it is kind to invite the committee, as well. 

# After the defense

Make sure to "clean up" the room and depending on the schedule of the week also put everything back as it was. The key usually can just be thrown in the dean's mailbox or handed in on the next day.
