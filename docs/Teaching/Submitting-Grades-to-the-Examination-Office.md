---
title: Submitting Grades to the Examination Office
---
This page summarizes the format and process for submitting grades to the examination office such that they can be entered into the official systems.

### Examination office

There are different people at the examination office responsible for the different study courses, e.g., Bachelor Informatik. Make sure that you know each student's study course and submit the grades to the right person via Mail.

### Which info needs to be sent

To submit a grade, you should send:
- The student's full name
- The student's Matrikelnummer
- The study course of the student (make sure this matches the responsibility of the examination office staff member you are contacting)
- The module identifier / name that the grade should be assigned for, e.g., INF-Sem for the Bachelor Informatik seminar module. 
- The grade

As of 18.08.2026 the file with the grades also needs to be signed.
