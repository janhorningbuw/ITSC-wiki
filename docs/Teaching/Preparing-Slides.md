This pages provides useful information about preparing slides for presentations.

# Being nice to your colorblind audience
Colorblindness affects a significant part of the population. Thus, the chance of having someone colorblind in your audience is quite high. This sections provides information on how to prepare your slides so that they are as effective for the colorblind in your audience as for everyone else.
- [Color schemes for colorblind](https://personal.sron.nl/~pault/)
- In case you indicate `good` and `bad` with the colors `red` and `green`, **always** use shapes like checkmarks and corrses to also indicate `good` and `bad`.

# Creating diagrams 
- The IACR has a list of TikZ diagrams for [crpyotgraphers](https://www.iacr.org/authors/tikz/) that you might want to considers using instead of creating your own. Also, be nice and cite them as your source if you use them.
- We also collect some helpful diagrams we created ourselves [here](https://git.uni-wuppertal.de/itsc-researchers/templates/-/tree/master/tikz-pictures).
