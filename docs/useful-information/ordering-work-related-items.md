# General procedure
To order work related items we cannot simply buy them of arbitrary online shops. The university has framework agreements (Rahmenvertäge) for specific item categories. A full list of EU framework agreements for the BUW can be found [here](https://dez1.uni-wuppertal.de/de/services/eu-vergaben-zoll-und-aussenwirtschaftsrecht/eu-rahmenvertraege/abrufe/) (only available in German).

1. Find a fitting framework agreement for your item
 * Some of the framework agreements have web shops available you can browse these even without a "Bestellberechtigten" account
 * If there are no fitting framework agreements do not worry, the item will then be ordered via "Beschaffungsantrag"
2. Write the most important points of your item to Jutta
 * For a hard drive that could be the capacity, size, speed, and ports
3. Wait until your items arrive
