# Vacation Carry-Over Regulations for Research Assistants

In contrast to the information provided [here](https://dez4.uni-wuppertal.de/de/abt-41/sgb-411-wirtschaftliche-belange-der-beschaeftigten-abwesenheitsangelegenheiten/urlaub/urlaub-tarifbeschaeftigte/), the university has aligned the collective agreement regulations (tarifrechtliche Regelungen) for Research Assistants (Wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter) with civil service regulations (beamtenrechtliche Regelungen).

## Automatic Transfer

* Remaining vacation days transfer automatically to the next calendar year.
* No formal application or action is required from your side.

## Expiration Deadline

* Transferred vacation must be fully used by March 31st of the second following year.
* Example: Vacation days from 2026 must be taken by March 31, 2028.
* Unused leave after this deadline expires completely without compensation.
