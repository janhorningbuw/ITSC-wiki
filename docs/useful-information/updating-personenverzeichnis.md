# Updating the "Personenverzeichnis"
## What is this?
The ["Personenverzeichnis"](https://www.uni-wuppertal.de/de/personen/verzeichnis/) (personal directory / list of people) helps other university people find you. This can be convenient if someone needs to contact you and only remembers your name. The entry regarding yourself can contain fields like

* room number,
* phone number,
* mobile phone number,
* email
* etc.

**NOTE:** To change the "Personenverzeichnis" u have to use a VPN or being otherwise in the University network. 

## Updating your personal information
If you need to update *your own* personal information on the "Personenverzeichnis", then [this page](https://zim.uni-wuppertal.de/de/hilfe/anleitungen/telefonverzeichnis/pflege-durch-mitarbeiter/) page offers a small tutorial in German. This link will open a page called "Pflege Telefonverzeichnis durch Mitarbeiter/in". The steps to changing are pretty straight forward.

0. Make sure you are in the University network
1. Change your data using this page [https://userdb.uni-wuppertal.de/tel_email/angestellte/](https://userdb.uni-wuppertal.de/tel_email/angestellte/)
2. Click on the button "Personendaten selbst pflegen". It should be relatively far at the top of the page
3. Another browser window should pop up prompting for your username.
   * Use your ZIM account to login
   * **Important** if you do not interact with this window for 90 seconds it will automatically close
4. You should now see a page with a form where some of the fields are already filled out
5. Insert the desired changes and finish the process by click on the button "Änderung der Mitarbeiterdaten melden"
   * For the "Raum" (room) field a specific pattern is required, which is as follows `<Building>.<Floor>.<Room Number>`. The floor number needs be two digits, `FME.02.08` for Jutta's office for example
6. Check if you received an email about the changes. In case you updated your email address check the "old" email for confirmation
