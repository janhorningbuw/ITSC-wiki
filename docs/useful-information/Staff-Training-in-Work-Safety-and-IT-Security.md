# General Information

As members of the university, we are legally required to complete annual training in work safety and IT security. The university provides self-study materials for both areas. Completion of the training and submission of the corresponding proof are mandatory.

# Work Safety Training

To complete the occupational health and safety training, please visit: https://uni-wuppertal.agu-hochschulen.de/ablauforganisation/unterstuetzende-prozesse/aus-und-weiterbildung/unterweisungen/.

You may need to log in first. Under "Vorgehensweise und Dokumente," select "Dokumentensammlung." The section entitled "Unterweisungsfilm" contains two videos, one of which has English subtitles. Please watch either of these videos.

# IT Security Training

To complete the IT security training, please enrol in the following Moodle course: https://moodle.uni-wuppertal.de/course/view.php?id=48603.

Please work through all the course materials and complete the final test. If you score more than 60% of the maximum number of points, you will receive a PDF certificate.

Unfortunately, the course materials are available in English only. Non-native speakers are encouraged to use an automated translation tool, such as their browser’s built-in translation feature or a browser extension.

# Proof of Completion

After completing both training modules, please send an email to gellert@uni-wuppertal.de containing:

1. the PDF certificate from the IT security course; and
2. a brief statement, for example in the body of the email, confirming that you watched and understood the work safety video and stating the date on which you watched it.
