Overtime refers to working hours performed at the employer’s instruction (Department 4) that exceed the hours scheduled or customarily established for the week within the regular working hours of full-time employees and that are not compensated by the end of the following calendar week. Overtime must be ordered by the employer (Department 4).

The employee should submit a written application for flexitime. This will be signed by Tibor and then filed internally. If there is an accident during flexitime and the health insurer has queries, we can present this.

A simple form for this can be found [here](https://uni-wuppertal.sciebo.de/f/980431243)

As a general rule, overtime must be compensated by corresponding time off. This compensatory time off must be granted by the end of the third calendar month following the month in which the overtime was accrued, but preferably by the end of the following calendar month.

Additional work applies to part-time employees who perform work beyond the workload individually agreed with them. As long as the regular working hours of a full-time employee are not reached or exceeded, such additional work does not constitute overtime subject to overtime premiums. Instead, it is paid at the applicable pro-rata rate or compensated by time off. Only once the regular working hours of a full-time employee are exceeded does overtime also arise for part-time employees.

Requests for overtime or additional work must be submitted in writing by the responsible line manager to Department 4 / Division 4.1.1.

For further information, please contact the Staff Council.
